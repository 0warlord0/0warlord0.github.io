Learning to code with an engineer's eye

2026 Nov 01

As a civil engineer, I'm used to thinking about what a thing has to do before worrying about how it looks. A drawing can be neat and still miss the point if the structure doesn't work. Coding has started to feel familiar in that way.

I'm still a hobby learner, and I make plenty of mistakes. But debugging has a nice rhythm to it: state what I expect, check what actually happened, then change one thing and test again. It feels a lot like tracing a problem on site, only the clues are in the browser or the terminal.

I like that code lets me turn a small annoyance into a small tool. The best project doesn't need to be clever. If it saves a bit of time and I understand how it works, that's a good result.
