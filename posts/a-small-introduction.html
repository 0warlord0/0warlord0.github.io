Why I'm starting this little blog

2026 Nov 01

I keep collecting small thoughts in random places: a note on my phone, a file on my laptop, a message I send myself and never open again. Starting a blog is my attempt to give some of those thoughts one home.

I'm a civil engineer from coastal Karnataka, and lately I've been learning to code for the joy of making things work. I like the moment when a vague idea turns into something useful, whether that's a simple webpage or a better way to think through a problem.

I'm not aiming for a grand project here. Just short notes on what I'm learning, what I'm building, and the ordinary things that seem worth remembering. If I can look back later and see how my thinking changed, that'll be enough.
