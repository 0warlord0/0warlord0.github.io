A dashboard for the parts of life I forget

2026 Nov 01

I enjoy making little systems, maybe because engineering trains you to notice how many moving parts sit behind something that looks simple. Lately I've been curious about putting a few useful parts of my own life on one dashboard.

Not to optimize every minute or turn life into a spreadsheet. More like a clear window: a few reminders of what I'm working on, what I want to learn, and what I shouldn't let slip just because the week got busy.

The tricky bit is deciding what belongs there. A dashboard should make the important things easier to see, not become another thing that needs constant attention. I'm still figuring that out, which is probably why it's worth writing down.
